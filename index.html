Courier Guy does not currently require users to create an account, sign in, or directly provide personal information in order to play.

Certain technical information may be processed by the operating system, application distribution platform, or technologies used to operate the game.

The current Closed Beta version of Courier Guy does not contain advertising or in-app purchases.

These features may be introduced in future versions of the game.

If advertising, in-app purchases, analytics services, or other third-party services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the relevant data practices.

Courier Guy is distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play and Apple services.

These platforms may process information independently according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Courier Guy is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Mad Budgie Games does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 through the game.

If we become aware that personal information belonging to a child under the age of 13 has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to address the issue.

6. Data Retention

Courier Guy does not currently operate a user account system or a game server intended to store users' personal information.

If this changes in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
