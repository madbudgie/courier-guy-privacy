This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Courier Guy.

Courier Guy does not currently require users to create an account, sign in, or directly provide personal information in order to play.

The current Closed Beta version of Courier Guy does not contain advertising or in-app purchases.

Advertising, in-app purchases, or other monetization features may be introduced in future versions of the game.

If these features are introduced and affect how user information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Courier Guy is distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play and Apple services.

These platforms may process information independently according to their own privacy policies, terms, and legal obligations.

5. Age and Younger Users

The current Courier Guy Closed Beta is intended for participants who are 13 years of age or older.

Age-related information is requested during the beta application process, and applicants who identify themselves as being under 13 are not knowingly selected for participation.

The target age groups for future public versions of Courier Guy have not yet been finalized.

This Privacy Policy and related platform disclosures may be updated as the game develops and as the intended audience changes.

6. Data Retention

Courier Guy does not currently operate a user account system or a game server intended to store users' personal information.

If this changes in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
