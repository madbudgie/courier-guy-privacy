Courier Guy Privacy Policy

Developed and operated under the Mad Budgie Games name
Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and deleted when you use Courier Guy.

Courier Guy is developed and operated under the Mad Budgie Games name.

The game uses certain services provided by Google, including Firebase and Google AdMob, and may use Sign in with Apple on supported platforms.

1. Accounts and Authentication

Courier Guy uses Firebase Authentication.

When the game is launched for the first time, an anonymous Firebase account is automatically created. This account is associated with a unique user identifier (UID) that allows game progress and server-side data to be associated with the correct player.

Players may optionally link their account to supported sign-in providers, including Google or Apple.

Depending on the authentication provider and the information made available by that provider, Firebase Authentication may process or store information such as:

  • A unique account or provider identifier
  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Authentication provider information
  • Account creation and sign-in information
  • IP address and technical information used for security and authentication

Courier Guy does not use authentication profile information for advertising profiles or social features.

When Sign in with Apple is used, Apple may provide the player's name and email address. Players may choose Apple's private email relay feature, in which case a relay address may be provided instead of the player's personal email address.

2. Cloud Saves and Game Progress

Courier Guy uses Google Cloud Firestore to provide cloud saving and account-based game progress.

Cloud save data may include:

  • Vehicle states and owned vehicles
  • Owned equipment, upgrades, and parts
  • Player energy and in-game day information
  • Game settings
  • Delivery and gameplay counters
  • Application version
  • Save timestamps and synchronization information

This information is associated with the player's Firebase UID.

The Cloud Firestore database used by Courier Guy is configured in the europe-west8 region (Milan, Italy).

3. In-Game Economy and Server-Side Records

Certain game economy information is stored and validated on the server in order to maintain game progress, synchronize accounts, and reduce fraudulent or unauthorized changes.

This may include:

  • Courier Lira (CL) balance
  • Star balance
  • Owned vehicles
  • Owned parts, upgrades, and other items
  • Economy transaction type
  • Transaction amount
  • Transaction timestamp
  • Catalog or economy configuration version

Server-side functionality may be processed using Firebase and Google Cloud services, including Cloud Functions.

4. Analytics

Courier Guy uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and to improve gameplay, stability, and future development.

Analytics information may include:

  • App installation or app instance identifiers
  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Country or general region
  • First launch information
  • Session information
  • Gameplay or app interaction events
  • Session and usage duration

Courier Guy does not intentionally send names, email addresses, precise location, chat content, contacts, photos, or similar directly identifying content to Firebase Analytics.

Where consent is legally required, including applicable users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, Analytics and advertising-related processing is controlled according to the player's consent choices.

5. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Courier Guy uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and technical problems.

Crash and diagnostic information may include:

  • Crash reports and technical stack information
  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Crash time
  • Firebase or Crashlytics installation identifiers
  • Technical diagnostic information related to the crash

Crashlytics information is used for diagnostics, stability, debugging, and fixing technical problems. Courier Guy does not use Crashlytics information for advertising personalization.

6. Advertising and Rewarded Ads

Courier Guy uses Google AdMob.

The game currently uses optional rewarded advertisements. Players choose whether to watch these advertisements in exchange for an in-game reward.

Courier Guy does not currently use banner advertisements or forced interstitial advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads services may process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Device and operating system information
  • General location inferred from information such as IP address
  • Advertisement impressions, interactions, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and security

Courier Guy does not request access to GPS or precise device location for advertising.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Courier Guy uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required to present privacy and consent choices before advertising-related processing is enabled.

Where required, players can reopen privacy options from the game's settings and change their available privacy choices.

Personalized advertising is used only where applicable and permitted by the player's consent and platform settings.

On iOS, Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization may also be requested where applicable. If tracking permission is denied, advertising identifiers such as IDFA are not made available for tracking through the applicable ad request.

Reward Verification

Rewarded advertisements use server-side verification to reduce fraudulent reward claims.

For this purpose, a pseudonymous player identifier such as the Firebase UID and a single-use verification value may be included in reward verification information sent through Google's advertising systems.

When an advertisement reward is verified, the server may store:

  • The reward timestamp
  • The reward amount
  • The advertising unit involved
  • Google's reward transaction identifier
  • A single-use verification value
  • A daily rewarded-ad counter

Additional information about how Google processes information in apps using its services is available at: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Google's Privacy Policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

7. Application Integrity and Fraud Prevention

Courier Guy may use Firebase App Check together with platform integrity services such as Google Play Integrity to help determine whether requests originate from an authentic version of the application and to help prevent abuse, tampering, automated attacks, or unauthorized access.

These services may process device or application attestation information and temporary integrity tokens required to perform these checks.

8. Information Stored on the Device

Courier Guy stores certain information locally on the player's device so that the game can function correctly and preserve progress.

Local information may include:

  • Signed local save files
  • Game settings
  • Older local save backups
  • Cached balance or game state information
  • Temporary pending-operation information
  • Pending rewarded-ad verification information, including UID, single-use verification value, and timestamp

Local files remain on the device unless they are removed by the game, deleted through an account deletion process, cleared through operating system controls, or removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to platform behavior.

9. Device Permissions and Information We Do Not Request

Courier Guy requires internet access and may include permissions or platform services required by Firebase, Google Play services, and advertising technologies.

The game does not currently request access to:

  • Precise GPS location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • User photos or media library

Courier Guy does not currently provide player-to-player chat or messaging.

In-app purchases are not currently enabled.

10. Third-Party Service Providers

Courier Guy relies on third-party services to provide authentication, cloud storage, analytics, diagnostics, advertising, security, and platform functionality.

These services currently include:

  • Google — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase App Check, Google Play Integrity, Google AdMob, and related Google services
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and applicable Apple platform services

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, contractual obligations, and legal requirements.

Apple privacy information is available at: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ .

Firebase privacy and security information is available at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

11. Data Retention

Courier Guy keeps data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to technical, security, legal, and third-party service requirements.

  • Account, cloud save, balances, and owned items: retained while the account exists and deleted as part of the account deletion process.
  • Economy and rewarded-ad transaction records: retained while the account exists because they are used for account integrity, balance verification, and dispute investigation, and deleted as part of the account deletion process.
  • Daily rewarded-ad counters: retained for up to 30 days.
  • Inactive anonymous accounts: anonymous accounts that have not been used for approximately 24 months may be deleted automatically.
  • Google or Apple linked accounts: are not automatically deleted solely because of inactivity.
  • Firebase Analytics user-level and event-level data: configured for a 2-month retention period. Google's standard aggregated reports may not be subject to the same retention control.
  • Firebase Crashlytics reports: generally retained for approximately 90 days in accordance with Firebase's service retention practices.
  • Courier Guy server logs: retained for up to 30 days unless a longer period is reasonably required for security, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.
  • Local save backups: remain on the player's device until removed by the player, application, account deletion process, or operating system.
  • AdMob information: retained by Google according to Google's own policies and applicable product settings.

12. Account and Data Deletion

Players can request deletion of their Courier Guy account and associated game data.

Deletion from Inside the Game

The account deletion option is available from: Main Menu → Settings → Delete Account.

An internet connection is required. The game uses a confirmation process before deletion and informs the player that progress, balances, stars, owned vehicles, owned items, and other associated progress will be permanently lost.

When an account deletion request is completed, Courier Guy initiates deletion of active account-related data under its control, including:

  • Firebase account association
  • Cloud save data
  • Game economy balances
  • Owned vehicles, items, and upgrades
  • Economy transaction records
  • Rewarded-ad records and account-related counters

Local save and cache information associated with the deleted account is also cleared. If the player continues using the game, a new anonymous account may then be created.

Deletion Without Access to the Game

Players who no longer have the game installed or cannot access their account can request account deletion through the Courier Guy account deletion page:

Courier Guy Account Deletion

Players may also contact us by email using the address listed in the Contact Us section below.

Residual Service Data

Certain information may remain temporarily in third-party backup, security, diagnostic, or logging systems after an account deletion request has been processed.

Examples include:

  • Courier Guy server logs, which may remain for up to 30 days
  • Crashlytics diagnostic information, which may remain for approximately 90 days
  • Analytics user-level and event-level information according to the configured 2-month retention period
  • Authentication or backup information retained temporarily by Firebase under Google's service retention practices
  • Advertising information independently retained by Google according to Google's policies

Deleting a Courier Guy account does not delete a player's separate Google or Apple account.

13. Younger Users

Courier Guy is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 13 through Courier Guy.

If we become aware that information has been collected from a user who should not have been using the service under applicable age requirements, appropriate steps may be taken to delete the relevant account and information.

14. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with Courier Guy.

These measures include account-based access controls, server-side validation for certain game systems, application integrity checks, and secure communications provided by applicable platform and service providers.

However, no electronic transmission, software system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Courier Guy develops or as technical, platform, legal, or regulatory requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
