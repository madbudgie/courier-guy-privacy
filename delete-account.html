Courier Guy Account and Data Deletion

Developed under the Mad Budgie Games name
Last updated: September 9, 2026

This page explains how users of Courier Guy can request deletion of their game account and associated personal data.

Courier Guy is developed and operated under the Mad Budgie Games name. Mad Budgie Games is used as the name of the development team and project and does not represent a separate legal entity.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to courierguygame@gmail.com with the subject Courier Guy Account Deletion Request.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your Courier Guy account whenever possible.
  3. Include your in-game Player ID or account UID if it is available. Do not send your password, payment card details, or other sensitive credentials.
  4. If necessary, we may request limited additional information to verify account ownership before completing the deletion.

Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days after account ownership has been verified. We will confirm by email when the request has been completed or if additional information is required.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Where associated with the verified account, deletion may include:

  • The Courier Guy account and authentication record
  • Account profile information and linked sign-in identifiers
  • Cloud save data, game progress, owned items, and virtual currency records
  • Account-specific gameplay and support data under our control

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted game progress, items, and virtual currency cannot be restored.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary to meet legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or platform requirements. This may include transaction records that must be retained by an app store or payment provider.

Aggregated or de-identified analytics and diagnostic information that can no longer reasonably be linked to your account may also be retained. Information controlled independently by Google, Apple, Meta, or another third-party provider is subject to that provider's own deletion and retention practices.

Facebook Login

Removing Courier Guy from your Facebook account or revoking Facebook Login access may stop future access through Facebook, but it may not automatically delete Courier Guy data already associated with your game account. To request deletion of that data, follow the email instructions above.
